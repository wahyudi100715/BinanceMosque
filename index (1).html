Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How do I know my funds are safe?+
All funds are held in a verified smart contract at 0xD61C022523AD0dd4924234144C572c5e16e68620 on BNB Chain. The contract code is open source and verified on BSCScan. No single person can move funds without the blockchain recording it permanently. You can verify every transaction yourself.
What is the withdrawal process?+
Withdrawals are milestone-based. The project owner can only withdraw funds as construction milestones are reached (15%, 35%, 55%, 80%, 100%). Every withdrawal is recorded on-chain with a transaction hash visible to everyone on BSCScan.
What wallet do I need to donate?+
You need any BNB Chain compatible wallet such as OKX Wallet, MetaMask (with BNB Chain network added), Trust Wallet, or Binance Web3 Wallet. Make sure you have BNB on BNB Smart Chain (not BEP-2) to donate. Minimum donation is 0.001 BNB.
Can I see the mosque plans and progress?+
Yes! Construction design renders are shown on this website. Physical progress updates will be posted on our social channels. All financial transactions are permanently viewable on BSCScan — the blockchain is our public ledger.
Is there a refund policy?+
The smart contract includes an emergency refund mechanism. If the funding goal is not reached by the campaign deadline, the owner can initiate refunds to donors. All such actions are recorded on-chain and transparent.
Are there any fees?+
Zero hidden fees. 100% of your donation goes directly into the smart contract. The only cost is the BNB Chain gas fee (typically less than $0.10) which goes to the network validators, not to us.
